Who We Are

At The Puzzl Co, we believe that the best learning happens through play.

Founded in 2024, we are a marketplace-first brand specializing in thoughtfully designed puzzles and educational toys for young children. Our team of puzzle experts and toy designers creates products that combine fun, learning, and child-safe design to help children build essential skills while enjoying every moment of play.

From first words and problem-solving to creativity and imagination, every product is crafted to make learning engaging and meaningful.
